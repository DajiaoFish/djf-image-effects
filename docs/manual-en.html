DJF-Image effects · Effect Manual

Transparent subject → 15 Illustrator-style effects

Pure-code output (Pillow + NumPy) — no AI image generation, no watermark, fully reproducible. All effects keep the transparent background by default, ready to drop into collages.

11
Halftone Lines

Radial Blur

13

glass

How to use: Send me a transparent-background subject image with a line like "turn this image into XX", and I'll output it using that effect's default parameters.

Tunable parameters: cell (dot / block size), strength (blur intensity), threshold (stamp threshold), bg=white (switch to white background), etc. — just specify per image when needed.
